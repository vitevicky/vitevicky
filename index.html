About us

Know your enemy: identification & treatment

Effective pest management starts with understanding what you're up against. Our licensed technicians identify the exact species affecting your property, then design a targeted treatment plan rather than a one-size-fits-all spray. From subtle termite signs to rodent entry points, we combine field experience with modern, low-toxicity methods to remove infestations at the source — and keep them from coming back.

Every visit follows a documented process: inspection, identification, treatment, and follow-up — so you always know exactly what's happening on your property and why.

Pest library

Know what you're dealing with

Browse common household and commercial pests — how to identify them, and how we treat them.

Termites

Silent structural destroyers that feed on wood, often undetected until damage is severe.

Identification
Mud tubes, hollow-sounding wood, discarded wings
Treatment
Liquid soil barrier & bait station systems

Cockroaches

Resilient pests that spread bacteria and trigger allergies in kitchens and bathrooms.

Identification
Droppings, musty odor, nighttime sightings
Treatment
Gel baiting & crack-and-crevice treatment

Rodents

Mice and rats chew wiring and insulation while contaminating food storage areas.

Identification
Droppings, gnaw marks, scratching sounds
Treatment
Exclusion sealing, trapping & bait stations

Mosquitoes

Disease-carrying insects that breed rapidly in standing water around the property.

Identification
Buzzing at dusk, standing water nearby
Treatment
Larvicide, fogging & source reduction

Bed bugs

Elusive nighttime biters that hide in seams and travel easily between rooms.

Identification
Rust-colored spots, itchy bite clusters
Treatment
Heat treatment & targeted residual spray

Ants

Colony-based invaders that contaminate food and can damage structures over time.

Identification
Visible trails, small dirt mounds outdoors
Treatment
Colony baiting & perimeter barrier spray
